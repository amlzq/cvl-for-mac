**Privacy Policy** (last updated: 2026-11-14)

This policy explains how the macOS desktop version of Chinese Variants Linter handles your data.

**What the app reads**

- With the Accessibility permission you grant, the app reads the text, caret position and character positions of the **currently focused text field**, to check Chinese script and regional wording and to draw marks under problems.
- For global shortcuts it needs the Input Monitoring permission, used to recognise your configured shortcuts, the suggestion popup keys, paste actions and whether an input method is composing. The app **does not record** what you type.
- “Check selection” and “Convert selection” simulate copy and paste and briefly use the clipboard. Your original clipboard is restored afterwards.

**What the app does not read**

- Password fields (secure text fields): nothing is read, checked or displayed.
- Apps you turned off in Settings: their text is not read.

**How data is handled**

- Text is used in memory on your Mac only for detection and then discarded. It is not written to disk.
- The app **does not connect to the network** and uploads no text. It has no analytics, ads or crash reporting.
- Settings, allowed words and custom phrases are stored locally in the app’s preferences. Exported dictionary files are yours to keep.

**Revoking permissions**

You can revoke Accessibility and Input Monitoring at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Checking stops working afterwards.
